Fozer Memorial Hospital is a regional inpatient hospital with 2000 employees. It stores HIPAA protected patient records and eomployee payroll data. Its real email domain is fozermemorial.org and the staff portal is portal.fozermemorial.org. Key departments are IT, HR, Billing and Pharmacy. Staff use Microsoft 365, a payroll system called PayGet, and a ticketing system called AssistPoint.
